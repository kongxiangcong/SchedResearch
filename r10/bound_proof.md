# Lower-bound direction and scope

All bounds constrain completion of a fixed payload DAG under any EXT arbitration with unchanged per-cluster outstanding Q, transport capacity, stages and dependency edges. They use graph work and the exogenous supply, never a policy's endogenous trace. Changing mapping, packetization or Q requires recomputation.

1. R9 aggregate bound remains unchanged.
2. Credit residence: each packet occupies one credit from acceptance to visibility for at least latency + EXT bytes/E + local bytes/D + visibility, since its two service stages are serial. Sum these minimal residence times per cluster / min(config outstanding, hardware outstanding, transport slots). This follows by integrating occupancy <=Q from 0 to completion. Ignoring all queuing/reservations weakens the bound.
3. Blackout starvation: during a clipped EXT blackout interval of length l in [0,T], no new read packet finishes EXT except at its left boundary. At most Q read packets can already have EXT data; their total remaining local work is <=B, the sum of the Q largest read-packet local byte counts in that cluster. Thus local read service within that interval is <=min(D*l,B). Outside blackouts it is <=D*time. Allow ALL write-local bytes free, i.e. omit them from this read-service inequality. Therefore necessary read bytes R <= D*T - sum(max(0,D*l-B)). This RHS is nondecreasing. Its first crossing of R is a lower bound on T. Credit held until visibility is essential. Clipping a blackout starting before zero gives extra imaginary buffered data, which is optimistic. No borrowing the same trace's idle intervals. This captures serial EXT-to-DMA starvation lost by max(aggregate work).
4. Dependency bound: process DAG in topological order; earliest command release >=max predecessor earliest completion. A DMA command needs at least max(total EXT work with absolute supply starting at release+latency, total local work starting at release+latency, longest individual packet's serial EXT+local work starting at release+latency), then visibility. For read commands local aggregate additionally cannot start before minimum packet EXT work has completed; for writes EXT aggregate cannot start before minimum packet local work. Omitting interference with every other command and intra-command credit limits only accelerates execution. Compute nodes take their declared duration. Max output earliest completion is a lower bound.

Take max of these inequalities, not their sum. Neither validates new hardware capabilities nor proves whole-model numerical equivalence. Bound gaps are upper bounds on recovery, never actual recovery.
